# Code of conduct

RelayProbe is committed to a professional, inclusive, and technically rigorous
community. Participation in project spaces requires respectful communication,
good-faith collaboration, and care for other people's safety and privacy.

Acceptable participation includes constructive review, clear disagreement about
technical choices, acknowledgment of uncertainty, responsible handling of
security information, and respect for contributors of every background and
experience level.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, disclosure of private information, credential sharing, and use
of project spaces to coordinate unauthorized network activity are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other project
material that violates this policy. They may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community or project. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary
disclosure of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security reporting channel. Reports will be handled with discretion; complete
confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when investigation or legal obligations
require limited disclosure.
